Summits, and what reached the ground

Since Andhra Pradesh was divided, its governments have held five investment summits and announced ₹45.97 lakh Cr across 2,722 agreements. Every figure on this page is the state's own, quoted from a budget speech or a survey you can open. The harder question is the second one, and the state has answered it far less often than it asked to be congratulated on the first.

Where the numbers for the same summit disagree

Partnership Summit 2025: the Budget Speech 2026-27 says 610 MoUs, ₹13.25 lakh crore, 16.13 lakh jobs. Akashvani's closing-day report said 400 companies, ₹11 lakh crore, 13 lakh jobs. Both are shown; we do not pick one.

Two things widely described as summits that we could not confirm

"Sunrise AP Investment Meet, June 2015" does not appear in any government document; the name attaches to the January 2017 summit. Davos 2025 and 2026: the state published no totals.
